Prometheus School — transport, as it actually runs

3 min

Children are scanned after they have sat down, not as the bus pulls up. The attendant works along the seats once the bus is already moving. So every morning tap is recorded some minutes after the bus actually reached the stop. That gap is the scan lag, written L.

Nobody has ever measured it. It is somewhere between 0 and about 8 minutes. This slider lets you choose a value and see the consequences immediately. It is not a correction factor — it is an admission that one number in this analysis is unknown, and a way to see how much it matters.

It matters enormously. At L = 0 around fifty stops look systematically late. At L = 6 only a handful do, and dozens look systematically early instead. The picture inverts. Until someone stands at three stops with a stopwatch for a week, every morning figure here is a range of possibilities, not a fact.

Afternoon numbers are unaffected. The drop-off tap happens as the child steps off the bus and the dismissal tap as they leave the premises. Both are honest. Anything on the Afternoon tabs can be acted on today.

Morning lateness is therefore an upper bound — the bus is never later than it looks here, and probably earlier. Morning ride durations are a lower bound.
